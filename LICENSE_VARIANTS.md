# Choosing the Right Reasoning License

The **Authorship Reasoning License (ARL)** defines three licensing paths for distributing reasoning modules that are technically separate from large language models (LLMs).

These licenses are modeled after widely accepted software and AI research licenses, with a specific focus on modular reasoning logic.

---

## 🚀 Herbert License (ARL-Permissive)

**File:** `Herbert.txt`

- Modeled after: MIT License
- Best for: Open source projects, broad adoption, community collaboration

**Features:**
- Fully open, including for commercial use
- Requires visible attribution
- No redistribution restrictions
- No patent litigation protection
- Technical separation from LLM weights is required

---

## ⚖️ Asimov License (ARL-Balanced)

**File:** `Asimov.txt`

- Modeled after: Apache 2.0 + Meta LLaMA license principles
- Best for: Teams who want attribution, protection, and responsible commercial use

**Features:**
- Allows commercial and academic use
- Attribution required
- Patent grant included, unless litigated
- Must document modifications if redistributed
- Clear technical separation from foundation models

---

## 🔒 Heinlein License (ARL-Restricted)

**File:** `Heinlein.txt`

- Modeled after: Meta LLaMA Community License + PyTorch Research License
- Best for: Research-only logic, prototypes, or unpublished work

**Features:**
- Non-commercial research use only
- Redistribution is prohibited without written permission
- Commercial licenses must be negotiated separately
- Strict separation from LLM weights required
- Attribution required for all demonstrations or publications

---

## 🧠 What Qualifies as a "Reasoning Module"?

These licenses apply to:
- Prompt templates and chain-of-thought files
- Symbolic rules and logical inference graphs
- Reasoning adapters (e.g. LoRA deltas)
- Decision trees, ReAct policies, and agent workflows

They do **not** apply to:
- Base LLMs, model weights, or tokenizers
- Training data or full fine-tunes
