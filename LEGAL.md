# Legal Overview – Authorship Reasoning Licenses

This project provides open-source **reasoning logic** that is separate from large language models (LLMs) such as LLaMA, GPT, or Mistral.

## Scope of the License

The licenses (Herbert, Asimov, Heinlein) apply only to:

- Prompt templates
- Chain-of-thought scaffolds
- Reasoning adapters (LoRA or delta)
- Agent logic or symbolic policies

They do **not** apply to:

- Foundation model weights, embeddings, or tokenizers
- Training datasets or fine-tunes

## Attribution Requirement

All public or production use must visibly include:

> “Reasoning powered by Authorship – https://authorship.com”
